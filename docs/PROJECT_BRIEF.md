# SignalDesk — product brief

## Purpose
Build a reusable GTM strategist for companies, initially SaaS/tech and B2B agencies. It should research available evidence, connect the findings and propose creative, commercially relevant experiments in a clear visual dashboard. It also serves as a portfolio/interview demonstration. PostHog and Stratabeat are examples, not hardcoded definitions of the product.

## Default workflow
1. Discover the company website through navigation (including dropdowns), sitemaps and relevant linked pages; inspect actual content. Supplement with search without claiming a complete Google index.
2. Inventory existing services, proof, offers, messaging and brand behavior before diagnosing gaps. Research competitors and relevant external customer discussions/reviews; preserve positive and opposing evidence.
3. Separate supported public facts, attributed claims, explicit experiment hypotheses and unknowns. Never invent missing company facts. Without CRM access, propose hypotheses rather than diagnosing funnel failure.
4. Create a small portfolio, normally 3–5 useful experiments. Fewer strong ideas are better than filling a quota. Consider SEO, paid media, email, outbound, community, partnerships, sales, retention and expansion; do not force a campaign into every channel.
5. Present the decision: what we found, why it matters, what to test, channel, pipeline mechanism, proposed resource cost, timing, success/stop criteria and relevant sources.
6. Longer-term: connect authorized CRM/channel data to improve diagnosis and measure outcomes. Execution is part of the ambition, but external execution is not implemented merely because a plan exists.

## Presentation
- Plain English, compact sections and visual comparisons. Avoid PMM jargon, huge paragraphs and oversized headings.
- The dashboard is the deliverable. Raw crawl inventories and system QA are secondary, opt-in details under Evidence/methodology.
- Sources should support each recommendation, not overwhelm the overview.
- Budget follows the scoped idea, not invented revenue-based CAC. Distinguish cash from internal time. Illustrative economics must be labeled; never invent actual lift, baseline or ROI.
- PostHog: witty, unconventional and playful, without excessive profanity. Stratabeat: professional, credible, imaginative; not PostHog humor with a new logo.

## Creativity is a requirement, not a wording pass
Reject a concept if it is simply a renamed normal agency activity, generic audit, testimonial, playbook, podcast, case study, client review, sales demo or cross-sell deck. Those formats can be ingredients, but need a materially different interaction or mechanism.
Before presenting, answer:
- What happens that would not happen in normal agency work?
- Why would the intended buyer voluntarily participate?
- Which evidenced company asset makes it credible and distinctive?
- How can it lead to a commercial next step?
- What evidence or cheap test would reject it?
Do not equate weirdness with relevance or a catchy title with originality. Evidence supports the rationale, not a guarantee that a novel idea works.

## Explicit rejected directions
- Claiming Stratabeat lacks proof: it has prominent testimonials and searchable/service-filtered case studies.
- Showing a long crawl report as the main deliverable.
- Presenting ordinary account management or service previews as creative experiments.
- Claiming an incomplete, hand-curated example is a completed autonomous SignalDesk run.
- Spending repeatedly on research already saved.

## Coverage nuance
The user expects thorough website research before recommendations. Missing content is not evidence of absence. A desired improvement is claim-specific treatment of gaps rather than blocking every hypothetical experiment because one page is incomplete. This is not authorization to label incomplete research complete or silently remove execution safeguards. The current implementation and remaining work are in WORKING_STATE.md.
