## Mandatory use in strategy work

1. Read the [operating standard](../process/GTM_OPERATING_STANDARD.md) and current client evidence first. Unknown client facts stay unknown.
2. Select **3–5 relevant entries**; do not load the whole library into every prompt. Prefer inspected substantive material over a description. Retrieve the original source again when freshness or precise interpretation matters.
3. Record source IDs under **method inspiration**, separately from company evidence. Reading a method does not validate a client's problem.
4. Combine an evidenced buyer question, a distinctive participation mechanism, a distribution route and a measurement method. Explain what someone actually does differently. A new name for a standard webinar, audit or account review fails the creative test.
5. Challenge the proposed experiment: what does the company already do, who benefits, why participate, what could disprove the idea cheaply, and what observation earns the next investment?
6. Present the short finding and experiment, with evidence on demand. Do not show the entire reference catalog or research process in the main dashboard.

## Financial reasoning

Use company data only when supplied or supported. A methodology article is not a source of this client's conversion rate, contract value or budget.

- Cost: itemize cash spending separately from internal time, with a real quote or explicitly proposed spending cap. A cap is a decision, not a known cost.
- Pipeline: count distinct qualified opportunities and their supported potential values; this is not revenue.
- Bookings: signed contract value; do not label a speculative pipeline calculation as signed business.
- Revenue: depends on delivery and recognition timing. Do not derive it solely from leads or traffic.
- Incremental impact: requires a credible comparison, not merely channel attribution. Avoid double-counting the same opportunity across channels.
- When inputs are missing, show the equation and missing inputs, plus the next observation needed. Do not fill the blanks with an industry average. A separate numerical teaching example requires an explicit illustrative label and must never become client data.

## Coverage and limits

The collection is a reusable foundation, not an exhaustive archive or a guarantee of creative quality. Founder narratives, vendor reports and selected campaign examples can suffer from commercial bias, selection bias and incomplete methods. Date checks prevent old examples from being relabeled as new trends. Provider diversity does not establish independent corroboration.

No full copyrighted books or paid courses are copied here. No accounts were subscribed or paid APIs called to build this expansion. Referenced course availability and commercial details may change.

**Runtime status:** Codex reads this library through repository instructions. The deployed SignalDesk application does not yet automatically retrieve these Markdown files; saving the files does not implement a retrieval system or validate generated ideas.

## Refresh and maintenance

The existing weekly refresh should start here, select a small unresolved topic, inspect original sources, and update the relevant collection. Favor a new mechanism or correction over volume. Preserve stable IDs; do not renumber old citations. Check for duplicate URLs and repeated mechanisms, access scope, source dates, relevance and limitations. Keep the [core refresh log](GTM_LIBRARY.md#refresh-log) as the common change record.
