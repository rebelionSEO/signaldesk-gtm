# Books and durable GTM methods

Verified/accessed: **2026-09-28**. These 15 entries extend R01–R18 in [the main library](../GTM_LIBRARY.md). They are original author, author-organization or publisher sources, opened during this review. They are **not full-book summaries**. The inspected material is specified for each entry. No books were purchased, restricted content accessed, forms submitted or paid APIs used.

Applications below are SignalDesk's synthesis. A method is not evidence about a client. Keep client facts unknown until supported; keep proposed tests visibly separate from observations. Do not convert an author's promotional claims into benchmarks. Select two or three relevant mechanisms, rather than quoting this whole file into every run.

## B01 — Value Proposition Design

- **Author/source:** Alexander Osterwalder and coauthors; [Strategyzer book page](https://www.strategyzer.com/library/value-proposition-design-2).
- **Date/access:** Page dated 2014-11-01; confirms 2014 edition. Public synopsis and companion-resource descriptions inspected; book/sample PDF not read. Accessed 2026-09-28.
- **Method:** Design and test the connection between an offer and what customers want, using a visual value-proposition model.
- **SignalDesk application:** Compare a company's documented promise with buyer interview evidence. Turn an unsupported value claim into a specific comprehension or relevance test before polishing copy.
- **Limit:** A filled canvas is not validated customer understanding. “Fit” cannot be inferred from the website alone.
- **Tags:** positioning, ICP, offer, research, message-testing.

## B02 — Business Model Generation

- **Author/source:** Alexander Osterwalder and Yves Pigneur; [Strategyzer book page](https://www.strategyzer.com/library/business-model-generation).
- **Date/access:** Publication date not displayed. Public description inspected; offered 72-page preview not accessed. Accessed 2026-09-28.
- **Method:** Model how a business operates so alternatives can be compared systematically, rather than only changing promotional tactics.
- **SignalDesk application:** When campaign ideas feel interchangeable, examine a different purchase route, delivery arrangement or partner role. List operational dependencies before calling it feasible.
- **Limit:** A new commercial mechanism may require product, delivery or legal changes outside marketing authority. Do not invent a company's cost structure.
- **Tags:** business-model, partnerships, offer, creativity, finance.

## B03 — Continuous Discovery Habits

- **Author/source:** Teresa Torres; [author's launch article](https://www.producttalk.org/continuous-discovery-habits/).
- **Date/access:** Launch article dated 2021-05-19, announcing availability. Article read; book not read. Accessed 2026-09-28.
- **Method:** Start with an outcome, discover opportunities through interviews, and test uncertainties in candidate solutions. Visualize the relationship between the outcome and possible interventions.
- **SignalDesk application:** Present one buyer question and competing tests, not a long unexplained idea backlog. Check the smallest consequential uncertainty first.
- **Limit:** Product-team methods require adaptation to agency GTM. A proposed experiment hypothesis is allowed; invented client facts are not. The source's habit cadence is not a mandatory research delay.
- **Tags:** discovery, research, experimentation, prioritization.

## B04 — Demand-Side Sales 101

- **Author/source:** Bob Moesta with Greg Engle; [The Re-Wired Group's book description](https://therewiredgroup.com/learn/demand-side-sales-101/).
- **Date/access:** Publication date not displayed. Public description read; book not read. Accessed 2026-09-28.
- **Method:** Understand how a customer makes progress and buys, rather than merely improving a seller's feature presentation.
- **SignalDesk application:** Interview buyers about the situation that triggered a search, alternatives considered, and what prevented a decision. Use those accounts to test a different buying experience.
- **Limit:** A job title or industry is not a buying situation. Without interviews or authorized sales evidence, the reason for stalled deals remains unknown.
- **Tags:** JTBD, ICP, sales, buying-process, research.

## B05 — Made to Stick

- **Authors/source:** Chip Heath and Dan Heath; [authors' book page](https://heathbrothers.com/books/made-to-stick/).
- **Date/access:** Source explicitly states release in 2007; exact date not stated. Public description read, not the offered first chapter or book. Accessed 2026-09-28.
- **Method:** Explore what helps an idea attract attention and remain understandable and memorable, including disrupted expectations and curiosity.
- **SignalDesk application:** Replace abstract claims with a small demonstration of the promised change. Test unaided understanding and recall, then assess whether it encourages a relevant next action.
- **Limit:** Memorability does not establish truth, qualified demand or revenue. A surprising headline alone does not satisfy SignalDesk's creativity test.
- **Tags:** creativity, message-testing, storytelling, comprehension.

## B06 — The Power of Moments

- **Authors/source:** Chip Heath and Dan Heath; [authors' book page](https://heathbrothers.com/books/the-power-of-moments/).
- **Date/access:** Publication date not displayed on inspected page. Public description read; embedded audio not played and book not read. Accessed 2026-09-28.
- **Method:** Deliberately design brief experiences around insight, connection, pride or elevation instead of leaving memorable moments to chance.
- **SignalDesk application:** Prototype a moment in evaluation or onboarding where buyers discover something useful themselves. Evaluate demonstrated understanding or progress, not just entertainment.
- **Limit:** Do not disguise ordinary client reviews as creative experiences. A memorable event does not fix weak delivery or prove retention impact.
- **Tags:** creativity, onboarding, buyer-experience, retention.

## B07 — Contagious

- **Author/source:** Jonah Berger; [author's book page](https://jonahberger.com/books/contagious/).
- **Date/access:** Publication date not displayed. Public description read; gated resources and full book not accessed. Accessed 2026-09-28.
- **Method:** Examine the reasons information travels through word of mouth and social transmission, including B2B contexts.
- **SignalDesk application:** For any shareable artifact, specify who would forward it, to whom, why doing so helps them, and how a recipient benefits without a sales call.
- **Limit:** Do not promise virality or treat shares as opportunities. Validate actual recipient relevance and voluntary sharing; an unrequested referral scheme is not automatically useful.
- **Tags:** creativity, distribution, referrals, advocacy.

## B08 — Monetizing Innovation

- **Authors/source:** Madhavan Ramanujam and Georg Tacke; [Wiley's authorized chapter-one excerpt](https://catalogimages.wiley.com/images/db/pdf/9781119240860.excerpt.pdf).
- **Date/access:** Publication date not established from this excerpt. Public 12-page chapter excerpt inspected; not full book. Accessed 2026-09-28.
- **Method:** Investigate perceived value and willingness to pay before committing to a fully developed offering; the excerpt illustrates the principle through automotive examples.
- **SignalDesk application:** Before recommending a new service package, test which outcomes and constraints matter to buyers and whether they will make a meaningful commitment.
- **Limit:** Stated willingness to pay may differ from purchase behavior. Historical illustrative cases are not causal proof or B2B pricing benchmarks. Never choose a price from an unrelated company's result.
- **Tags:** pricing, packaging, offer, finance, experimentation.

## B09 — Blue Ocean Strategy

- **Authors/source:** Chan Kim and Renée Mauborgne; [official book page](https://www.blueoceanstrategy.com/books/blue-ocean-strategy-book/).
- **Date/access:** Exact publication date not displayed; page describes a 20th-anniversary enhanced edition. Public description read; book not read. Accessed 2026-09-28.
- **Method:** Consider changes to market boundaries and sources of demand rather than only competing on the same attributes.
- **SignalDesk application:** Ask which buying constraints prevent otherwise relevant people from participating, and whether a different engagement structure could remove one. Compare the resulting mechanism against existing offers.
- **Limit:** “Uncontested” is not an evidence-backed description of a market until investigated. A new category name is not proof of demand or defensibility.
- **Tags:** differentiation, positioning, creativity, category, offer.

## B10 — Hacking Growth

- **Authors/source:** Sean Ellis and Morgan Brown; [Penguin Random House description](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/545936/hacking-growth-by-sean-ellis-founder-of-growthhackerscom-and-morgan-brown/).
- **Date/access:** Publisher states 2017-04-25. Public description inspected; full book/sample not read. Accessed 2026-09-28.
- **Method:** Coordinate cross-functional, repeated tests across acquisition, engagement, retention and repeat purchase rather than relying on isolated large campaigns.
- **SignalDesk application:** Connect a small acquisition test to the next customer action and an explicit learning decision. Assign owners across the handoff instead of evaluating channels independently.
- **Limit:** Historical growth-company stories are not transferable forecasts. Speed without tracking quality, sufficient evidence or delivery capacity can produce noise.
- **Tags:** experimentation, channels, growth, retention, activation.

## B11 — This Is Marketing

- **Author/source:** Seth Godin; [author's explanation of the book](https://seths.blog/2018/11/what-is-inside-thisismarketing/).
- **Date/access:** Article is in November 2018 archive; exact book publication date not established here. Public article read; book not read. Accessed 2026-09-28.
- **Method:** Define a specific audience worth serving and the change the work is intended to create, rather than maximizing broad attention.
- **SignalDesk application:** Explain who benefits from an experiment and why they would participate before deciding format or channel. Validate that group instead of silently inventing an ICP.
- **Limit:** An attractive audience story is still a hypothesis without customer evidence. Narrowness by itself does not establish commercial viability.
- **Tags:** ICP, positioning, permission, brand, relevance.

## B12 — The JOLT Effect

- **Authors/source:** Matthew Dixon and Ted McKenna; [Penguin Random House description](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/697165/the-jolt-effect-by-matthew-dixon-and-ted-mckenna/).
- **Date/access:** Publisher states 2022-09-20. Public description read; study dataset and book not inspected. Accessed 2026-09-28.
- **Method:** Distinguish interest in changing from confidence in making a purchase; the authors frame indecision and fear of failure as potential barriers.
- **SignalDesk application:** Where sales evidence supports uncertainty, test a concrete decision aid or reversible first step against simply adding more benefit claims.
- **Limit:** The publisher's reported study is not independently audited here. Do not assume every stalled deal reflects indecision; budget, fit, timing and procurement are competing explanations.
- **Tags:** sales, message-testing, risk, buying-process, conversion.

## B13 — Scaling Innovation

- **Authors/source:** Madhavan Ramanujam and Eddie Hartman; [Simon-Kucher interview with Hartman](https://growthblueprint.simon-kucher.com/issue-02/qa).
- **Date/access:** Publication date not displayed; footer copyright is not treated as publication proof. Full public interview read, not book. Accessed 2026-09-28.
- **Method:** Consider acquisition, monetization and retention together; communicate delivered value and investigate reasons for churn rather than reflexively discounting.
- **SignalDesk application:** Explain how an experiment might affect new business, ongoing delivery and renewal. Identify the required cost, margin and customer inputs before computing financial outcomes.
- **Limit:** Consultancy interview promotes the authors' services. Price increases and expanded services are not justified merely by this framework. Routine retention contact is operational hygiene, not automatically creative GTM.
- **Tags:** finance, pricing, retention, expansion, value.

## B14 — The Customer Success Economy

- **Authors/source:** Nick Mehta and Allison Pickens; [Gainsight book page](https://www.gainsight.com/resource/the-customer-success-economy-book/).
- **Date/access:** Exact publication date not established. Page refers to four years after its 2016 predecessor; do not substitute that for a verified release date. Public description read; gated chapter not obtained. Accessed 2026-09-28.
- **Method:** Treat customer success as a concern across the organization, rather than confining it to a post-sale department.
- **SignalDesk application:** Check whether an experiment's acquisition promise is supported by onboarding and delivery. Tie expansion proposals to customer progress, and specify evidence needed to establish a renewal problem.
- **Limit:** Vendor has a commercial interest in customer-success investment. Without customer data, do not assign health scores or claim churn risk. More meetings are not a creative retention strategy.
- **Tags:** retention, expansion, handoffs, customer-success.

## B15 — Trustworthy Online Controlled Experiments

- **Authors/source:** Ron Kohavi, Diane Tang and Ya Xu; [Cambridge University Press contents page](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/trustworthy-online-controlled-experiments/contents/E5D69C46E095D864BE242CE5BF5FEEBE).
- **Date/access:** Publisher lists online publication 2020-03-13 and print year 2020. Public metadata/contents inspected only; full chapters unavailable in this review. Accessed 2026-09-28.
- **Method scope:** Contents distinguish introductory experimentation, complementary/alternative techniques, platform construction and advanced analysis. This is a deeper technical reference to consult when a quantitative test is justified.
- **SignalDesk application:** Separate “we can learn from a small prototype” from “we can estimate causal lift.” Escalate to an appropriately designed controlled test only when data and feasible sample support it.
- **Limit:** This entry is a reading pointer, not a summary of unseen statistical guidance. Do not derive sample sizes or significance rules from a table of contents.
- **Tags:** experimentation, measurement, causality, evidence-quality.

## How these should affect creativity

Use the methods to vary the mechanism: a different buying constraint (B04/B12), participation experience (B06), reason to share (B07), commercial arrangement (B02/B08), or audience boundary (B09/B11). Then apply the existing creative rejection tests. A book-inspired label on a webinar, report, demo or review is still routine work unless the participant behavior materially changes.
