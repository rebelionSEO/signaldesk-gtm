## Sessions and episodes

### W01 — AI-Assisted Creativity: 6 B2B Marketers Share How AI Fits Into Their Process
- Source: [Exit Five / Walker Sands](https://exitfive.com/live/ai-assisted-creativity-6-b2b-marketers/).
- Date/access: event dated 2026-09-25; public event description inspected. Recording not reviewed; page still contains registration language after the event date.
- Scope: six marketers describe creative workflows and where human judgment remains necessary.
- Application: seek concrete examples of how an idea was challenged and changed, not just the tool or prompt used. Ask what human decision prevented generic output.
- Limit: the page does not disclose the actual workflows or their commercial results. Do not attribute invented techniques to speakers.
- Tags: creativity, AI, human-review.

### W02 — Paid Ads Playoff
- Source: [Exit Five session page](https://exitfive.com/live/paid-ads-playoff-proven-b2b-paid-ads-plays-that-turn-spend-into-pipeline/).
- Date/access: 2026-07-10; event description and public replay link inspected, recording not reviewed.
- Scope: short ad examples, questions and audience voting. The organizer promises discussion of pipeline results.
- Application: use the format to review competing experiment proposals: show the actual creative, ask for the buyer action, challenge the measurement, choose a small test.
- Limit: audience votes measure preference, not incremental pipeline. Organizer claims of results are not verified campaign evidence.
- Tags: PPC, creative, pipeline, experiment-review.

### W03 — B2B Email Marketing: What Still Works?
- Source: [Exit Five / Knak](https://exitfive.com/live/b2b-email-marketing-what-still-works/).
- Date/access: 2026-05-15; public description and FAQ reviewed, recording not reviewed.
- Scope: personalization, production workflows, campaigns and reporting email impact to leadership.
- Application: evaluate email as a relevant next step after an organic, paid or event interaction. Define the recipient's reason to act and the resulting qualified conversation, not only opens.
- Limit: campaign examples and result calculations are not available in the inspected description. Email effectiveness must be tested with permission, deliverability and account-level context.
- Tags: email, distribution, lifecycle, pipeline.

### W04 — The Marketing Playbook for Keeping Customers
- Source: [Exit Five / Customer.io](https://exitfive.com/live/the-marketing-playbook-for-keeping-customers-not-just-acquiring-them/).
- Date/access: 2026-05-01; public description reviewed, recording not reviewed.
- Scope: behavior-triggered engagement across email, push, SMS and in-app channels; choosing relevant channels.
- Application: propose one customer-value trigger and an appropriate intervention, then measure the value event and later retention separately.
- Limit: do not import the page's generic acquisition-versus-retention cost multiplier into client finances. Customer behavior and actual churn causes require first-party data.
- Tags: retention, expansion, activation, lifecycle.

### W05 — The B2B Buying Experience With AI: What Changes?
- Source: [Exit Five session](https://exitfive.com/live/the-b2b-buying-experience-with-ai-what-changes/).
- Date/access: 2025-10-30; public description reviewed, recording not reviewed.

- Application: investigate what prospects already know at the first conversation, which claims they trust and what missing information prevents a decision.
- Limit: a panel premise does not prove this client's buyers use AI or that an AI visibility campaign will create revenue.

### W07 — Unlocking Retention and Growth: How to Set Customer-led KPIs (Part 2)
- Source: [Forget the Funnel — Georgiana Laudi and Claire Suellentrop](https://forgetthefunnel.substack.com/p/unlocking-retention-and-growth-how).
- Date/access: 2024-03-05; substantive episode notes and timestamp summaries reviewed. Audio/full transcript not reviewed.
- Method in public notes: distinguish first value, value realization and continued value; choose measures connected to the customer's desired outcome.
- Application: pair a behavioral success measure with a later commercial measure. For example, completing a valuable workflow is different from merely logging in; the actual workflow must be established with customers.
- Limit: do not repeat the introductory signup-abandonment statistic as a benchmark; underlying study was not inspected. Value events cannot be invented from a marketing page.
- Tags: KPI, activation, retention, customer-research.
